Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription? On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Six-Pack Stack. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

How do I get a refund for an App Store purchase? Apple handles those at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account? In the app: You tab, then Delete my account. It removes your plan, logs, check-ins and photos.

My steps aren't counting. Open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Motion & Fitness, and turn on Six-Pack Stack.

How does 1:1 coaching work? Apply in the app or at six-pack-stack.vercel.app. I read every application myself.
