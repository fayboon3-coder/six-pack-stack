I've tried coaching before. Why is this different?

Most coaches hand you a generic plan and tell you to buy more stuff. I start by cutting what doesn't work, then build everything around your foods, your schedule and your gym, and I adjust it every week.

How does the app fit in?

Your whole plan lives in the Six-Pack Stack app: workouts with form videos, your meals, your check-ins and your progress. As a coaching client you get every week unlocked. I see your check-ins and send your changes, and you can text me on WhatsApp any time.

Will I have to give up foods I like?

No. Your plan is built from what you already eat. You can still eat out, and the app tells you what to order.

I'm busy. Will this fit my life?

Workouts on the days you choose, at the gym or at home, and a 2-minute check-in on Sunday. If you have a job and a social life, this was built for you.

Do I have to buy a bunch of supplements?

No. Most guys end up buying less. You get the short list that actually works and cut the rest.

What if I travel or have a crazy week?

Text me. I'll adjust your plan for that week so you stay on track.

Tell me on your application. You'll need your doctor's OK first, and I'll build around their advice.
